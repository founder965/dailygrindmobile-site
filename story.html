Our Story

It started on my fridge.

Daily Grind started while I was studying at Harvard with an Expo marker and a to-do list written on my refrigerator.

At first, it was just a place to keep track of everything I needed to get done. But one long list wasn't really working. Over time, I started breaking it into sections for things like my top priorities, self-care, cleaning, and everything else I needed to keep track of that day.

I kept changing it based on what actually worked for me and what didn't.

The fridge eventually became a daily worksheet. The worksheet grew into a physical workbook, with space for planning, tracking, and scratch paper. I kept using it, changing it, and adding things whenever I found myself wishing the system did something differently.

Eventually, I wanted the same system on my phone.

That became Daily Grind.

The app is still built around the same idea as the original list on my fridge: one simple place to see what matters today, keep track of the everyday things that matter too, and pick up tomorrow without starting all over again.
